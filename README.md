## 🕹️ How to Play FrontWars

### Getting Started

At the start of the FrontWars game, you'll choose a starting position on the map. From this small base, you need to expand your territory by clicking on adjacent areas. But expansion isn't without risk - each expansion disperses your military forces, making existing territories more vulnerable to attack.

Balance is key to success in FrontWars. You need to find the right balance between expansion and defense, allocate population between troops and workers, and choose between economic development and military investment. Every decision affects your chances of victory in this strategic FrontWars experience.

### Basic Gameplay Mechanics

- **Territory Expansion:** Click on adjacent areas to expand your territory
- **Attack Ratio Management:** Control the percentage of troops sent out with each click using the slider in the bottom left
- **Troops vs Workers Balance:**
  - **Troops:** Used for attacking and defending in FrontWars
  - **Workers:** Generate gold for upgrades and special units

### Advanced Strategies

As the FrontWars game progresses, strategic depth increases significantly. Building cities can dramatically increase your population cap, allowing you to maintain larger armies and economies. Defensive structures protect your borders from invasion. Missile silos provide devastating long-range strike capabilities in FrontWars.

The naval system adds an entirely new dimension to FrontWars. After building ports, you can send warships to attack enemy coastal territories, deploy destroyers to capture enemy trade ships, and establish trade routes with allies to generate passive income. Controlling the seas is often a key factor in determining victory in this strategic FrontWars experience.
